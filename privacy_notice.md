## Digital Lean Manufacturing Lifecycle Assessment (Beta)

**Notice Version:** v1.0  
**Last Updated:** 1 August 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

The Digital Lean Manufacturing (DLM) Lifecycle Assessment is a research and development initiative conducted by the National Electronics and Computer Technology Center (NECTEC), a research center under the National Science and Technology Development Agency (NSTDA). The assessment aims to develop and evaluate a methodology for establishing a trusted understanding of the current state of a transformation initiative associated with a selected business goal, thereby supporting informed planning, decision-making, and subsequent transformation activities.

This assessment system is currently in its first beta release. The assessment methodology, software platform, and supporting processes remain under active development and evaluation and may change during the course of the research.

A separate document provides detailed information on the objectives, methodology, and scope of the assessment.

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

To administer the assessment, we may collect the following information:

- Company name
- User name
- Email address
- Assessment responses
- Assessment history and activity logs (such as response timestamps and user actions)

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data unless specifically required and communicated.

---

## 3. Purpose of Collection

The information is collected for the following purposes:

- Administering the assessment
- Authenticating users
- Recording assessment responses
- Supporting collaborative completion of assessments within participating companies
- Evaluating and improving the assessment methodology
- Improving the software platform
- Supporting research and development activities conducted by NECTEC

Assessment results may be analyzed in aggregate for research purposes.

---

## 4. Data Storage

The assessment system currently uses the following services:

- **Application hosting:** Streamlit Cloud
- **Database hosting:** Turso Cloud
- **Source code management:** GitHub

The project team implements reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information stored within these services.

---

## 5. Access to Information

Assessment information is accessible only to authorized members of the project team and authorized users from the participating company, as appropriate for administering the assessment and conducting the research.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to support the research project, evaluation of the assessment methodology, and any related reporting obligations, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law or NECTEC policies.

This assessment platform is a beta system.
